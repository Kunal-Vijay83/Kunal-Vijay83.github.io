I am a **Mechanical Engineering undergraduate at Netaji Subhas University of Technology (NSUT), Delhi**, with research interests spanning **robot learning, intelligent physical systems, learning-based control, and embodied AI & robotics**.

My work sits at the intersection of **mechanical systems, robotics, and machine learning**, with a particular interest in how learning-based methods can be used to build intelligent systems that operate and adapt in the physical world.

## Research

My current research focuses on **learning-based modelling and explainability for complex physical systems**. As part of my B.Tech. major project, I am developing a spatio-temporal graph learning framework for predictive maintenance in multi-component industrial systems, using the NASA C-MAPSS FD004 dataset.

The work explores how relationships between system components and temporal degradation patterns can be incorporated into machine-learning models, alongside explainability methods for understanding model decisions.

I am currently revising the research implementation and manuscript with the goal of developing the work into a publishable research contribution.

## Robotics & Engineering

My engineering experience includes both physical robotics systems and industrial engineering.

I co-led the development of an **FPV quadrotor for the ASME IAM3D competition**, working across mechanical design, 3D-printed structures, flight-control configuration, and a remotely actuated payload-gripping mechanism.

I have also worked on **student-designed sounding rockets**, including CAD, engineering analysis, flight-dynamics simulation, and supplementary trajectory modelling.

My industry experience includes internships at **Hero MotoCorp**, covering engine design and development as well as production engineering.
